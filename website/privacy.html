PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE: FEBRUARY 12, 2025

THE SHORT VERSION

Circle Filler collects absolutely nothing. No personal information. No analytics. No telemetry. No cookies. No ads. No third-party SDKs that phone home. Nothing.

DATA COLLECTION

Circle Filler does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data or usage data of any kind. Specifically, this application does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Cookies or tracking pixels
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

LOCAL STORAGE

All game data (scores, settings, game state) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the application.

NETWORK ACTIVITY

Circle Filler does not make any network requests during gameplay. The application functions entirely offline after installation.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Circle Filler does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no third-party SDKs that collect user data.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Because Circle Filler collects no data whatsoever, it is suitable for users of all ages. The application complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations worldwide by virtue of collecting no information from any user, regardless of age.

WALLET INTERACTION

Circle Filler may include optional Solana wallet connectivity for future features. Any wallet interaction is initiated exclusively by the user and processed on the public Solana blockchain. No private keys, seed phrases, or wallet credentials are ever stored or transmitted by the application.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we ever change this policy (unlikely, since there's nothing to change), the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

CONTACT

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the developer via the Circle Filler GitHub repository or the Solana dApp Store listing.

Summary: We don't collect your data. We don't want your data. Play the game. Fill the circles.
